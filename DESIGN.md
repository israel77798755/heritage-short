# Design brief and audience testing

This document records the intended audience, story purpose, shot rationale, and actual audience-test evidence. Unknowns and tests that have not happened are marked as pending rather than filled with assumptions.

## Project intent

- **Film:** *The Wisdom of Our Mothers (የእናቶቻችን ጥበብ)*
- **Story:** A fictional Ethiopian mother, Almaz, teaches her son, Dawit, four lessons through ordinary family and community moments.
- **Core lessons:** Trust God, respect others, never give up, and remain hopeful.
- **Format:** 75 seconds of story, followed by a 3-second AI disclosure end card; Amharic narration, English subtitles, and 9:16 and 16:9 exports.

The desired tone in the brief is warm, emotional, and cinematic. The storyboard is fictional and should not be presented as a historical account or as representative of every Ethiopian community.

## Audience and intended response

- **Intended audience:** Pending owner confirmation. The assignment requires a specific audience, but none has been confirmed in the project brief yet.
- **Where they will watch:** Pending. The project requires both vertical and horizontal exports.

## Shot rationale

Each shot has a reason to exist, recorded here alongside the storyboard's `purpose` field.

| Shot | Storyboard title | Reason |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Dawn Prayer and Morning Light | Establish the home, dawn mood, and the mother's quiet presence. |
| 2 | Trust God | Introduce trust in God through a shared prayer. |
| 3 | Peace in Faith | Show Dawit finding reassurance in his mother's steady faith and care. |
| 4 | Respect Every Person | Show respect through the mother's greeting and Dawit's help for an elder. |
| 5 | A Place at the Mesob | Reinforce humility and respect through hospitality at a shared meal. |
| 6 | Never Give Up | Let Dawit experience frustration and receive encouragement rather than an easy success. |
| 7 | Trying Again | Show Dawit choosing to try again, making perseverance visible. |
| 8 | Hope in the Storm | Contrast hardship with the mother's reassurance and a glimpse of light. |
| 9 | Light After Hardship | Resolve the storm beat with mother and child moving forward together. |
| 10 | Her Wisdom Lives On | Close the memory and show the mother's lessons continuing to guide the narrator. |

## Cultural and language review

The current storyboard uses an Ethiopian highland setting and named cultural objects. These choices need review by someone familiar with the represented context before final generation. Amharic narration and English subtitles also need a fluent Amharic speaker's review for meaning, natural phrasing, and pronunciation. Automated validation can check for Amharic characters and non-empty subtitles; it cannot establish translation quality or cultural accuracy.

The characters are fictional. Do not use a real person's face, voice, or identifying family details without documented informed consent. See [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md).
