## Post Copy

Growing up, some of the strongest lessons we carry aren't from textbooks—they are the quiet words our mothers repeat every morning before the day begins.

I created **The Wisdom of Our Mothers (የእናቶቻችን ጥበብ)**, a 78-second AI-assisted short film narrated in **Amharic** with **English subtitles**, telling the fictional story of an Ethiopian mother, Almaz, and her son, Dawit, through four everyday lessons:
1. **Trust God**
2. **Respect other people**
3. **Never give up**
4. **Remain hopeful, even during difficult times**

### Why This Story Matters to Me
I wanted to test whether modern AI filmmaking tools could carry an Ethiopian story—in Amharic, with warmth, dignity, and restraint—rather than generic templates.

### One Thing I Learned Building It
Generating one picture is easy; building a **reproducible pipeline** around a single `storyboard.json` contract is the real engineering work. Keeping character appearance consistent, aligning Amharic narration to exact shot timings, ducking an original instrumental score beneath the voice, and automating everything on GitHub Actions taught me far more about orchestration and failure recovery than prompting alone.
